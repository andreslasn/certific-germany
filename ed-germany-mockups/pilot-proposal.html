Digital patient intake in the Emergency Department

Certific is a digital intake platform for emergency departments. After triage, lower-acuity patients complete a structured, symptom-specific questionnaire on their own phone while they wait -in their own language. The treating clinician receives a structured history draft in German before the consultation begins.

No integration with your hospital information system is required to start.

What the pilot tests

Whether structured, patient-completed intake earns its place in the daily routine of your ED team:

  • Does it save clinician documentation time?
  • Does it improve the completeness of the patient history?
  • Does it work reliably for patients with limited German?

Pilot terms

Cost

Free of charge for the full pilot period. Pricing is discussed only if the hospital decides to continue after the pilot.

Duration

3–6 months (to be agreed), with a defined patient scope: ambulatory, lower-acuity patients after triage.

Onboarding

On-site visit at the start (setup, staff briefing, workflow walk-through), followed by remote support and a named contact person.

Exit

Either party may end the pilot at any time. No obligation to purchase.

Data protection

Certific is ISO 27001 certified and acts as data processor under a German-language data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) with technical and organisational measures annex. All data is hosted in the EU. The product is not a medical device: it collects and structures patient-reported information and does not triage, diagnose, or recommend treatment.

How success is measured

Agreed jointly at the start; our proposal:

  • Primary: clinician documentation time per patient case, compared with the pre-pilot baseline
  • Completeness: structured review of patient-reported histories against the treating clinician's final documentation
  • Uptake: share of eligible patients who complete the questionnaire during waiting time
  • Satisfaction: short patient and clinician ratings (1–5 scale) collected during the pilot

What we ask from the hospital

One condition matters more than any other: patients complete the questionnaire only when a staff member actively points them to it. Posters and QR codes alone do not work -this is a consistent finding from live ED deployments.

We therefore ask that:

  • A named role at triage or registration hands each eligible patient the invitation as a fixed step in the intake routine
  • A clinical contact person joins a short review call with us every 2–3 weeks
  • The ED shares anonymised baseline figures needed for the agreed success measures

Next step

A 60-minute working session with the ED leadership and nursing lead to fix the patient scope, the prompting step, and the success measures -then a start date.
